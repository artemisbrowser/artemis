And the rest.

  • Scout. Links from other apps open in a window of their own, so your spaces stay as you left them.

  • Archive. Tabs you haven’t used for 12 hours, or a time you choose, tidy themselves away. Pinned tabs and favourites stay.

  • Rules. Send a site to the space it belongs in, every time.

  • Live Folders. Your pull requests, issues and feeds, kept fresh in the sidebar.

  • Mini player. Video keeps playing in a small window that floats above the rest.

  • Profiles. Give a space its own sign-ins, cookies and history.

  • Incognito windows. Nothing kept when you close them, and a Touch ID lock if you want one.

  • Link shortcuts. ⇧-click to Peek, ⌘-click to open behind, ⌥-click to split.

  • Clean links. Copy Page Link drops the tracking tags from the address.

  • Web Inspector. Press ⌥⌘I to look inside any page.

  • AppleScript. Script your tabs and windows.

  • Eleven icon colours. Pick the app icon that suits your Dock.
