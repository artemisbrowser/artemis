Nothing to sign up for. Nothing sent about you.

  • Private by default.

    • No account and no telemetry.
    • History and tabs stay on this Mac. Passwords, passkeys and codes live in its keychain.
    • Besides the pages you open and the Live Folders you set up, Artemis goes online only to update its filter lists and extensions, to add uBlock Origin Lite on first launch, to check for its own updates every 4 hours, and to fetch a site’s icon from Google’s icon service when the site has none.
    • Search suggestions, error reports and the leaked-password check stay off until you turn them on.

  • Open source, so it stays.

    Every line is on GitHub under the GPL 3.0. Anyone can read it, build it and keep it going.
