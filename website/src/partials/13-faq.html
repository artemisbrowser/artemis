Questions.

What does it cost?

Nothing. There’s no paid version and nothing to sign up for.

Which Macs does it run on?

Any Mac with M1 or newer, on macOS 26 or later.

Why does macOS ask before I open it?

This build isn’t checked by Apple yet, so macOS stops it the first time. Open Privacy & Security in System Settings and click Open Anyway. You only do it once, and every build’s source is on GitHub.

What does Artemis collect?

Nothing about you. There’s no account and no telemetry. Here’s everything that goes online.

Can I bring what I have now?

Yes. Choose File › Import from Browser. Tabs, bookmarks and history come from more than twenty browsers, and sign-ins and passwords from many of them.

Do my extensions and password manager work?

Many extensions install straight from their store pages, and some don’t work yet. Bitwarden works, and it’s one of the suggestions in Settings › Extensions. Artemis also has its own passwords, passkeys and one-time codes.

What engine does it use?

WebKit, the engine built into macOS. For the odd site that turns WebKit away, the site’s settings in the address bar can make Artemis present itself as another browser.

How do updates work?

Artemis checks every 4 hours, downloads updates in the background and installs them when you quit. Every update is signed. You can turn automatic installs off in Settings › About.

Can I make it my default browser?

Yes. Click Make Default on the welcome screen, or Make Artemis Your Default Browser in Settings › General.

Who makes it, and will it last?

Fin makes it, on his own. The code is under the GPL 3.0, so anyone can build it and carry it on, and it needs no account or server of ours to run.

Where do I report a bug or ask for something?

On the Discord, or as an issue on GitHub.

Can I use the code?

Yes, under the GPL 3.0. A changed version needs its own name and icon, and a notice in its About window.
