Bring your extensions and passwords.

  • Extensions

    Install them straight from their store pages, where the button reads Add to Artemis. Bitwarden works, and it’s one of the suggestions in Settings › Extensions.

  • Passwords

    Artemis saves passwords in your Mac’s keychain and suggests strong ones. Passkeys and one-time codes are built in.

  • From your old browser

    Bring tabs, bookmarks and history from more than twenty browsers, and sign-ins and passwords from many of them. Bitwarden and 1Password exports work too. Choose File › Import from Browser.
