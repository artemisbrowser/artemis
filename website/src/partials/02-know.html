Get to know Artemis.

  • Spaces. Each space keeps its own pinned sites, folders and tabs. Swipe with two fingers to move between them.

  • Command bar. Press ⌘T and type a few letters. Artemis fills in the site you meant and lists the tabs you already have open.

  • Split view. ⌥-click a link and it opens beside the page you’re on. Up to four pages, tiled your way.

  • Recent tabs. Hold ⌃⇥ and your last tabs fan out as previews. Let go on the one you want.

  • Themes. Give every space a solid colour, a gradient or glass, and an emoji or symbol of its own.

  • Intelligence. Ask about the page you’re on and Apple’s on-device model answers, with nothing sent anywhere. Or open the side panel and hand a task to an agent.
