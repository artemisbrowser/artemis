Every tab has a place.

  • Spaces. Each space keeps its own pinned sites, folders and tabs, and can keep its own sign-ins. Swipe with two fingers to move between them.

  • Command bar. Press ⌘T and type a few letters. Artemis fills in the site you meant and lists the tabs you already have open.

  • Peek. ⇧-click a link and it opens over the page you’re on, without losing your place. Read it, then close it or keep it as a tab.

  • Split view. ⌥-click a link and it opens beside the page you’re on. Up to four pages share the window, tiled in rows and columns.

  • Recent tabs. Hold ⌃⇥ and the tabs you used last fan out as previews. Let go on the one you want to go straight back to it.

  • Themes. Give every space a solid colour, a gradient or glass, and an emoji or symbol of its own.

  • Intelligence. Ask about this page answers on your Mac, with nothing sent. Both it and the side panel’s agent are off until you set them up.
