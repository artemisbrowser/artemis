Ask the page. Only when you want to.

Turn on Ask about this page and Apple’s on-device model reads the page you’re on and answers, with nothing sent anywhere. It’s off until you turn it on, and it needs Apple Intelligence.

The agent in the side panel is separate. It works through an AI coding agent you already have on your Mac, under your own account, so its work goes online. It’s off until you set it up, and it asks before each step unless you tell it otherwise.
