Private by default.

Ads and trackers are blocked from the first launch, and fingerprints are scrambled site by site. There’s no account and no telemetry. Your history, tabs and passwords stay on your Mac.

Telemetry

Error reports stay off unless you turn them on.
Ads and trackers
Blocked
Rules run inside WebKit, so a blocked request never leaves your Mac. The lists update themselves every few days.
Fingerprint defence
On
Each site sees slightly different canvas, WebGL, audio and hardware readings, so those readings can’t link your visits across sites.
Copy Page Link
Cleaned
Copy Page Link (⇧⌘C) drops tracking parameters. So does Copy Link in the sidebar menu and Copy Page Link in the command bar.
Incognito windows
Can lock with Touch ID
Turn it on and they lock whenever you switch to another app. Nothing in them is saved.
Your data
On your Mac
History, tabs and settings are plain files in a folder you can open. Passwords live in your keychain.
On-device intelligence
Off until you turn it on
Optional helpers can tidy tab names and answer questions about the page you’re on, using Apple’s on-device model. It needs Apple Intelligence.
Passwords
Built in
With passkeys and verification codes.
