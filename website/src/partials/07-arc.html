Move in from anything.

Bookmarks, history and passwords come across from the other browsers on your Mac. Some only hand over passwords as a file you export. From Arc, the whole setup moves in: spaces, profiles, tabs, folders, favourites and archived tabs, your cookies, your newest 4,000 history pages and, if you tick them, your passwords.

macOS asks for Arc’s key before cookies and passwords come across. Arc is only read, and nothing there changes. Arc’s spaces arrive beside yours. Artemis offers the move when it first opens, and it’s always in File › Import from Arc. Extensions, Boosts and Easels don’t come across.
