Move in from Arc.

Artemis reads your Arc setup from your Mac. Spaces, profiles, tabs, folders, favourites and archived tabs come across. So do your cookies, your newest 4,000 history pages and, if you tick them, your passwords.

macOS asks for Arc’s key before cookies and passwords come across. Arc is only read, and nothing there changes. Arc’s spaces arrive beside yours. Artemis offers the move when it first opens, and it’s always in File › Import from Arc. Extensions, Boosts and Easels don’t come across.
