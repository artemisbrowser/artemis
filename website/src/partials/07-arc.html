Leaving Arc? Bring it all with you.

Arc stops working on 22 December. Artemis reads your Arc sidebar and rebuilds it, with your spaces, pinned tabs, folders, favourites and recent history where you left them. Your sign-ins come too, and your passwords if you tick them.

  • Same names

    Spaces, pinned tabs, folders, favourites, Peek, split view, the command bar on ⌘T, recent tabs on ⌃⇥ and Archive.

  • New names

    Little Arc is Scout. Air Traffic Control is Rules, in Settings › Links. Each Arc profile becomes an Artemis profile.

  • Stays in Arc

    Boosts, easels, notes and extensions. Add your extensions again from their store pages.

Three steps

  1. Download Artemis and open it.
  2. Choose File › Import from Arc, tick what you want and click Import.
  3. Make Artemis your default browser in Settings › General.

Arc is left as it was, and nothing leaves your Mac. macOS asks once before Artemis reads Arc’s sign-ins and passwords. Artemis isn’t made by or connected to The Browser Company.
