Garlic varieties to try

Hardneck garlic has a stronger flavour. Softneck garlic keeps for longer.

  • Hardneck. Stronger flavour, and a flower stalk to snap off in early summer.
  • Softneck. Milder, and it keeps through the winter.
  • Elephant garlic. Big, mild cloves. It’s closer to a leek.
