Betelgeuse, the red shoulder

Why the star at Orion’s shoulder looks orange, and why it dimmed in 2019.

Betelgeuse is a red supergiant, a star near the end of its life. Its cool surface glows orange, and you can see the colour without binoculars.

Late in 2019 it faded noticeably for a few months. Astronomers think a cloud of dust the star had thrown off was mostly to blame.
